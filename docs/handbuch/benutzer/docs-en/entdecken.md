# Discover Ablago

Ablago can do more than you see at first glance. So that you don't have to search, there are three quiet helpers: short **tours** when you visit a page for the first time, a **tip** in the assistant at the bottom right now and then, and the page **Discover Ablago**, where you can read everything at your own pace.

## The tours { #rundgang }

When you visit a page for the first time, a short tour shows you what is there – right on the real interface. Every tour goes the way you read: **from the top left to the top right, then on downwards** – the assistant at the bottom right comes last. Things that belong together in a workflow come one after the other.

| Tour | Starts by itself | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| **Your library at a glance** | on your first visit to the Library | logo, search, **+ New**, your menu at the top right, the views with *To do* on the left, the list in the middle, the [assistant](assistent.md) at the bottom right |
| **Working in a folder** | the first time you open a folder | what the folder is, the star, the **Folder** menu, the tabs, **Save folder** at the bottom |
| **My account** | never – the page only shows a sentence | back to the library, then the sections Account, Security, Appearance, Notifications, Absence, Report an error |
| **The Workbench at a glance** | on your first visit to the Workbench (only if you may administer Ablago) | logo, Library and Workbench, your menu, the areas on the left, users, the overview, **Customise Workbench**, the assistant |

![First step of the tour through the Library](bilder/rundgang.png)

Each step highlights the place it is about; the rest of the page is dimmed meanwhile. You cannot change anything by accident. The card with the text stays in its place – at the bottom centre on a computer, at the bottom above the assistant on a phone –, only the highlight moves. Only if the card would cover a place, such as the bar with **Save folder** on a phone, does it move up just far enough to leave the place free.

- **Next** and **Back** move through the steps; **Done** in the last step closes the tour.
- **Skip** ends it – it will not appear again by itself.
- **Later** closes it for now; the next time you sign in, Ablago offers it again. ++esc++ works like *Later*.

Only one tour starts at a time, and when a tour ends the next one does not follow straight away: it only comes when you open another page yourself. Anyone who may administer Ablago and lands in the Workbench after the setup therefore first sees the Workbench tour, and the Library tour on their first switch to the Library.

**My account** never starts by itself. On your first visit there is just one sentence at the top: “New here? A short tour shows you what you can set up here.” A click on the sentence starts the tour; **×** next to it hides the sentence.

On a phone the tours are shorter: the columns are stacked there, and Ablago only shows places that also exist on the phone.

Anyone who used Ablago before this update does not get the Library tour again; the new tours through a folder, My account and the Workbench appear once on the first visit. You find all tours at any time under **Discover Ablago → Tours**.

### Tours from packages { #paket-rundgaenge }

Some packages bring their own tours, for example “Contracts at a glance”. They lead you through a task and show the places on a real folder – the first folder of that kind you are allowed to see – or on an empty form, without saving anything. If there is no suitable folder yet, you only see a sentence about what to expect.

Such tours **never start by themselves.** In the **30 days** after installing or updating, Ablago offers them as a tip (“Contracts at a glance – a short tour”); all of them are under **Discover Ablago → Tours**. You only get tours for folders you are allowed to read. If the package brings the texts in your language, you see the tour in your language, otherwise in German.

The tours work with the keyboard: ++tab++ moves between the buttons and stays inside the tour, Enter presses the button. A screen reader reads out, for each step, where the highlighted place is. If “Reduce motion” is switched on on your device, the tour jumps from step to step without a transition.

## The tip of the day { #tipps }

The [assistant](assistent.md#tipps) at the bottom right shows the tip of the day under **Tips** – as a card, for example: “Is there an archive number on a paper? Type it into the search box at the top and press Enter to open exactly that document.”

- **At most one new tip a day.** A small dot on the assistant's button shows it; as soon as you have looked at the tips, the dot is gone. The tip stays the same until the evening. If there are several new things after an update, you see one with “… and 2 more” – not several cards.
- **Also from packages:** installed packages bring their own tips, such as “New in Contracts 2.1 – …” after an update; they count as “new” for 30 days. You only get tips about folders you are allowed to read.
- **Show me** takes you to the right place and highlights it briefly – or starts the tour or opens the matching page in this manual, such as [What buttons look like](knoepfe.md). If the place is in a folder, such as “Folder ▾” for the tip about reminders, Ablago opens the most recently changed folder you may read; if there is none yet, you only see a short sentence. **Next tip** moves on.
- **Only what you can use:** you do not get tips about things you are not allowed to do or that are not set up for you.

You can switch the dot for the tip of the day on and off at any time under [My account](profil.md#startseite) → **Appearance → Start page → Tip of the day**.

## The page “Discover Ablago” { #seite }

You reach it via the [assistant](assistent.md#tipps) at the bottom right → **Tips** → **All tips and tours ›**. It begins with the sentence “What else Ablago can do, explained briefly.” and has these sections – each only with what you may use:

- **New since your last visit** – only if something new has arrived with an update.
- **Tours** – Ablago's own and those of the installed packages; with **Start tour** you see one (again).
- **Tips** – all tips by area (Search, Post, To do and deadlines, Folders, My account), with a small search box. A click on a tip shows the place.

![Discover Ablago](bilder/entdecken.png)

Tips hidden earlier stay listed here, marked *hidden*; **Show again** brings them back into the assistant. Even if you have switched the tip of the day off, you find everything here.

## What Ablago remembers

Which tips you have seen or hidden and which tours you already know is stored only in your own Library, with your account. An app you have connected with an API key – even “read only” – reports the same; what you dismissed there does not come back in the browser. None of it leaves your Library – Ablago does not send usage data.

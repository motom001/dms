# Assistant {#assistent}

At the bottom right of every page there is a round button with a small **folder with a face**: the **assistant**. It opens a small window with three tabs – **Ask · Tips · Manual**. That way help is where you are working, and the home page stays calm.

![Assistant with the “Tips” tab](bilder/assistent.png)

- The assistant **never opens by itself**. It doesn't jump and doesn't wave.
- A small **dot** on the button shows that there is a new [tip of the day](#tipps). As soon as you have looked at the tips, it is gone.
- When you move the mouse over the button (or the keyboard focus is on it), the folder's flap folds back briefly and it winks – only then, without sound. If **reduce motion** is switched on on your device, nothing moves.
- **×** at the top right or **Esc** closes the window. With the keyboard you reach the button like any other; when it opens, the focus moves into the window, and when it closes, back to the button.
- On a phone the window opens as a **sheet from the bottom** across the full width. The button has its own strip at the bottom there and covers neither content nor the bar with **Save** – on a large screen too, as soon as there is such a bar. In the Workbench on a phone the button is at the top, in the header.

## Ask: Ask your Library {#frag-deine-ablage}

If your administrator has set up a package such as **AI assistant**, there is the **Ask** tab. Here you ask a question in your own words – for example "When does the car insurance run out?" – and get an answer with links to the matching folders. Without such a package there are only **Tips · Manual**.

The conversation is at the top of the window, the box below it. It stays when you change pages – it is only gone when you reload. Below it, a small line says which AI service writes the answer.

### Asking a question {#eine-frage-stellen}

- **Ask:** type your question, then **Ask** or Enter (new line with Shift + Enter). The answer appears as it is written; **Cancel** stops it.
- **Sources:** below the answer are the folders it comes from, as links. The numbers in square brackets, e.g. "[2]", point to the passages below.
- **"Sent to the AI: n excerpts from m folders":** expanded, you see exactly the passages that went to the AI service – never more than that, never your whole Library. Only folders that **you** may read are searched; passwords are never included.
- **"… 6 of 9 excerpts (shortened)":** the AI service can only read a certain amount of text at once. If it was too much, the package shortened it – expanded, you then only see the passages that really went there. In long chats it may also leave out older questions and answers; that is then noted below the answer. If something is missing from the answer, a **New chat** often helps.
- **Follow-up questions:** you can keep asking in the same chat ("And when is the notice period?"). For every question, Ablago looks for new passages. **New chat** starts from the beginning.
- **Nothing is saved.** The chat only stays in this browser window and is gone on reloading. Ablago's log only records *that* a question was asked – not what. For the cost overview Ablago only keeps, per question, who asked when, how long it took and how many tokens the AI service reported – again without question and answer.
- **"AI features paused until …":** your administrator has set a monthly budget for the AI service, and it has been used up. From the date shown it works again – or as soon as your administrator raises the budget.

The answer is written by an AI service. It can be wrong or fail to find something – check important things in the sources. The answer is only text: it doesn't change anything in your Library. The excerpts go to the AI service your administrator has set up – either a computer on your home network, then everything stays with you, or a provider. Which one it is your administrator sees under Workbench → Packages at the package's service address.

## Tips {#tipps}

The **Tips** tab shows the **tip of the day** as a card – at most one new one a day:

- **Show me** closes the window, opens the right page and briefly highlights the place (or starts a tour).
- **Next tip** moves on to the next tip.
- **See all tips ›** leads to [Discover Ablago](entdecken.md#tipps).

You switch the dot for the tip of the day on and off under [My account](profil.md#startseite) → **Appearance** with **Tip of the day**.

## Manual {#handbuch}

Under **Manual** you search the manual without leaving Ablago: type a word, and below it the hits appear with title, place in the manual and the words around the match. A click opens the place in a new tab; **Open the whole manual** at the bottom opens all of it.

- **In the Library** the assistant searches the **user manual**, **in the Workbench** the **admin manual**. The line above the hits says which one it is.
- It searches the manual that comes with your Ablago installation – **without the internet**, in the edition that matches your version.

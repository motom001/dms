**How the wizard continues:** Choose **Set up new** – or **Restore from a backup** if you are moving from another device. Then you enter the **name of your household** and create **your account** – with your email address and your password; it may administer Ablago. Then you say **what you use Ablago for** – home, work or both; for work straight away with your organisation. After that you add **packages** such as text recognition, and **optional** things like sending email follow. After "Set up now" you receive the **key and emergency sheet**. Every step has **? Help with pictures** at the top right; everything is described in detail in the [Administrator manual](https://help.ablago.app/admin/aktuell/en/installation/#der-einrichtungsassistent).

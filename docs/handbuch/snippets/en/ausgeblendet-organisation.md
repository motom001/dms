!!! tip "Area not in the sidebar?"
    This area is not in every level of the workbench – in the level *Beginner*, the default for private use, it is hidden. The table under Customise the workbench shows from which level it is there. You can show it under [Customise the workbench](https://help.ablago.app/admin/aktuell/en/uebersicht/#werkbank-anpassen), for everyone or just for you. **Hidden does not mean switched off:** the area keeps working, and its notices always appear in the overview.

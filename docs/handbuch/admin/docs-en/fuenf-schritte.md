# Set up in five steps {#einrichten-in-funf-schritten}

You've just installed Ablago and gone through the setup wizard? Then only a few things are missing so that your paperwork is safe and everyone can get going. Everything else you look up when you need it.

## 1. Switch on backup {#1-sicherung-einschalten}

The most important thing first: the backup. Under **Workbench → [Encryption](verschluesselung.md#notfallblatt)**, download the **emergency sheet** and keep it separately from the NAS, printed or in a password safe – a backup can only be opened with the key on it. Then switch on the **schedule** under **Workbench → Backup**; from then on Ablago backs itself up and checks every week whether the backup can be restored.

*You already downloaded the emergency sheet at the end of the wizard* – without it, "Finish setup" doesn't work. It also contains the **emergency code**, in case you ever lock yourself out. Only the schedule is still missing.

![Backup in the workbench](bilder/werkbank-sicherung.png)

More: [Backup](sicherung.md)

## 2. Invite users {#2-benutzer-einladen}

Under **Workbench → Users** you invite your family or your team; everyone gets an email with a link and sets their own password. Everyone is a **member** at first: they work in the Library and see everything. Who may see what you can restrict later via roles and [permissions](berechtigungen.md).

In the wizard you only created **your own account**. You work in the Library with it; you administer deliberately via **Administer Ablago** in the user menu. Now invite the others. **Ideally, a second person gets the permission to administer Ablago** – in case you're not around. The workbench overview reminds you of this too ("Good if someone else can administer …"); the person gets an invitation link, and then you tick **May administer Ablago** for them under *Users*.

![Users in the workbench](bilder/werkbank-benutzer.png)

More: [Users and roles](benutzer.md)

## 3. Choose packages {#3-pakete-wahlen}

Packages bring ready-made **folder types** – for contracts, insurance policies, vehicles, letting or the business. Under **Workbench → Packages** you see the catalogue; install what suits you. Many packages also bring **reminders**, for instance of a notice period or the next vehicle inspection. They work as automations; which are switched on you see under **Workbench → Building your Library → Automations**.

*Already done if you ticked something in the wizard's step "Packages" and set it up with "Set up now". The question "What will you use Ablago for?" before it already preselected suitable packages there – for home, for work or for both.*

![Packages in the workbench](bilder/werkbank-pakete.png)

More: [Packages](pakete.md)

## 4. Bring in post {#4-post-hereinholen}

For a start, **one** way is enough: on your phone you photograph a letter directly into Ablago (**+ File**), an **Intake folder** takes scans from the scanner, a **mailbox** fetches invoices from your e-mails. Everything lands as [post](https://help.ablago.app/benutzer/aktuell/en/post/) and is assigned to a folder from there.

![Assigning post](bilder/post-zuordnen.png)

More: [Intake folder](eingangsordner.md), [Mailboxes](postfaecher.md)

## 5. Done – and what you can leave for later {#5-fertig-und-was-du-liegen-lassen-kannst}

That's it. All other areas of the workbench have good defaults, and the **overview** speaks up when something needs doing – for instance when the backup is too old or space is running low. You don't have to set anything else. If you want more, you'll find everything in the table [What do you want to do?](index.md) in the overview.

**What Ablago sends out in the process:** only what you trigger or switch on – such as the invitations by e-mail. The full list is under [What leaves your Library](datenschutz.md#was-deine-ablage-verlasst).

More: [Workbench and overview](uebersicht.md)

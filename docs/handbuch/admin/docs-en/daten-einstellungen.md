# Settings for backing up

**Workbench → Back up data → Settings** gathers lasting settings that apply to the whole library. They used to sit in the middle of the workflows – only the workflows themselves stay there: starting and downloading a [full export](export.md), restoring and emptying in the [bin](papierkorb.md).

## What users may do themselves { #was-benutzer-selbst-durfen }

**Users may export their own data** switches *Export my data* under **My account → Account** on or off for all users. It is off by default. What the own export contains and what users see while it is off is described under [Export my data](export.md#meine-daten-exportieren).

The setting applies to everyone, also in a purely private household – which is why it is here and not under data protection.

# Procedure documentation (template) {#verfahrensdokumentation-vorlage}

Anyone in Germany who keeps books or has to keep records – including with a cash-basis accounting (Einnahmen-Überschuss-Rechnung), e.g. for freelance work, a small business or photovoltaics run as a business – needs a **procedure documentation** (*Verfahrensdokumentation*) under the **GoBD**: a description of how receipts are created, get into the system, are kept, protected and found again. The GoBD don't apply to purely private households; with letting or tradespeople's invoices, order doesn't hurt anyway.

Software alone is never "GoBD-compliant" – only the procedure as described and practised makes it so. Ablago **supports** this: history of every change, [versions](fassungen.md) of the settings (who changed folder types, permissions, views … when and how), checksums of the files, the [archive mode](archiv.md) with a deletion lock until the end of the period, versions instead of overwriting, permissions, encrypted backup with test restore.

## The template {#die-vorlage}

The template describes in ready-made wording what Ablago does, and marks everything you add yourself with **[square brackets]**. It is written in German, because the GoBD are German rules:

- [Word (.docx)](vorlagen/verfahrensdokumentation.docx)
- [LibreOffice (.odt)](vorlagen/verfahrensdokumentation.odt)
- [Markdown on GitHub](https://github.com/motom001/dms/blob/main/docs/handbuch/vorlagen/verfahrensdokumentation.md)

It is structured into:

1. **General** – who, which activity, which version and variant of Ablago
2. **Receipt intake** – paper, intake folder, mailbox, electronic receipts and e-invoices in their original format, assigning
3. **Replacement scanning** – device, checks, when the paper is destroyed, exceptions
4. **Filing and order** – folder types, required fields, views, retention periods, [archive mode](archiv.md) and "Retention expired"
5. **Traceability and immutability** – history of the folders, checksums, [versions](https://help.ablago.app/benutzer/aktuell/en/versionen/), Check all files, bin
6. **Access protection** – accounts, two-factor sign-in, sign-in protection, roles, permissions, read access for auditors
7. **Data backup and restore** – schedule, off-site copy, emergency sheet, test
8. **Operation** – updates, maintenance mode, log
9. **Data access during a tax audit** – export, interface
10. **Changes to the documentation**

## Good to know {#gut-zu-wissen}

- **No tax or legal advice, no certificate.** You create the procedure documentation yourself; the template is a starting point. Agree it with your tax adviser, especially periods and replacement scanning.
- **The procedure documentation is kept too** – with its earlier versions. File it e.g. as a folder in Ablago and enter every change in the table at the end.
- **It describes Ablago as it is today.** When new functions you use are added, extend the sections. The template here is updated with every version.
- Background: GoBD (letter from the German Federal Ministry of Finance, last amended on 14.07.2025); retention periods currently 8 years for accounting receipts, 10 years for books, 6 years for business letters, each from the end of the calendar year.
